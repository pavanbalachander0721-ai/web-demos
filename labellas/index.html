Questions

Do you ship outside Bangalore?

Yes, we ship across India. Delivery time depends on your pincode; we'll confirm it when you order.

What's the minimum for return gifts?

25 pieces. For weddings and large events, message us 2–3 weeks ahead so everything is made fresh.

Can I choose colours and fragrances?

Yes. Pick a fragrance when you add a candle, and for event orders we match colours to your theme.

How should I burn my candle?

On a flat, heat-safe surface, away from fans and children, for up to 2 hours at a time. Sculpted candles look just as lovely unlit.

How do I pay?

Once we confirm your order on WhatsApp, we'll share UPI or bank details.
