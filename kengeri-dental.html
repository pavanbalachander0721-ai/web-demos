Tooth pain sorted, gently.

Dr Mahanth Ashok explains every step before he starts. Here's what a root canal actually involves, so it stops being scary.

1 of 5

It starts with a toothache.

Usually decay has reached the nerve inside the tooth. That's the throb that keeps you up at night.

2 of 5

First, an X-ray.

The doctor finds exactly what's wrong and shows you, before anything is done.

3 of 5

Then it's numbed.

Treatment happens under local anaesthetic, so you feel pressure, not pain.

4 of 5

The infection is cleaned out.

The damaged nerve is removed and the canals inside the root are cleaned.

5 of 5

Sealed and capped.

The canals are filled and sealed, and a crown protects what's left. You keep your own tooth.
